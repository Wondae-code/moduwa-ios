2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE

moduwa helps people find travel destinations in South Korea they can actually get into. Public listings say a place exists, but not whether there is a ramp, an elevator, or a step at the entrance — the detail that decides whether a trip is possible for a wheelchair user, a parent with a stroller, or an older traveler.

We combine the Korea Tourism Organization's official barrier-free dataset (10,000+ verified locations) with traveler reviews, ranked so that reviews by people with the SAME accessibility needs appear first.

Audience: travelers with mobility, visual or hearing impairments; families with strollers; older travelers; and those who plan trips for them.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS

No account is needed for the core experience — browsing, search, place details, accessibility information and reading reviews all work signed out. Sign-in is only needed to write a post or review, create a plan, and save/like items. Demo credentials are in the Sign-In Information fields. Only ONE account type. No sample files, no paid content or in-app purchase.

Onboarding is three screens, no sign-up. On screen 2 you must pick an option before the button enables — 지체장애 (wheelchair), or 해당없음 (Not applicable) to skip — then 완료했어요 (Done). On screen 3 tap 둘러보기 (Browse) to enter signed out.

To sign in: Home → gear icon (top right) → 로그인 → "이메일로 시작하기" (Continue with email). Sign in with Apple also works; Kakao needs the KakaoTalk app, so please use email or Apple. If an email-verification screen appears, tap 나중에 하기 (Do it later) — it blocks nothing, so the demo mailbox is not needed.

Key screens, under the gear icon on Home, which opens 설정 (Settings):
• Account deletion: 설정 → 프로필 편집 → 회원 탈퇴
• Report / Block: any post or review → ⋮ (top right) → 신고 / 차단. Own content shows 삭제 (Delete).
• Blocked users: 설정 → 차단한 사용자 · Support: 설정 → 고객센터
• Main feature: set needs at 설정 → 내 무장애정보 편집, then set Home sort to 추천 (Recommended).

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES

• Korea Tourism Organization (한국관광공사) TourAPI — public open data: all place listings, barrier-free attributes and place photography. Attributed in-app at the bottom of 설정.
• Our own REST API on Railway with PostgreSQL — accounts, reviews, posts, comments, plans and user photos. Photos sit on our own server volume; no third-party storage.
• Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, Kakao Login. Kakao Maps SDK for maps. APNs for push, requested only when the user turns the toggle on at 설정 → 알림 설정.

Not used: no payment processor, AI service, analytics SDK, advertising SDK or tracking. Our privacy manifest declares NSPrivacyTracking = false, as do all three bundled third-party components. No location, camera or photo-library permission is requested — photos use SwiftUI PhotosPicker.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES

None. Distributed in South Korea only; behaviour is identical everywhere. No region-gated features, no region-specific pricing — the app is free.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY

Not a regulated industry — a travel information and community app. No medical, diagnostic or treatment information, and not a medical device: "ramp at the entrance" describes a building, not a person's health.

TourAPI data and photography come from Korea's national open data portal (data.go.kr) under terms permitting reuse, including commercial reuse, with attribution. We display that attribution in-app; documentation on request. All other content is written by us or submitted by our users under our Terms of Service (https://moduwa.app/terms).
