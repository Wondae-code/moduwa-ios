■ 2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE

moduwa ("모두와" — Korean for "with everyone") helps people find travel destinations in South Korea that they can actually get into.

The problem: public tourism listings tell you a place exists, but not whether there is a ramp, an elevator, an accessible restroom, or a step at the entrance. For a wheelchair user, a parent with a stroller, an older traveler, or someone with a visual or hearing impairment, that missing detail decides whether a trip is possible. Today they have to call each venue individually.

Target audience: travelers in South Korea with mobility, visual, or hearing impairments; families traveling with infants and strollers; older travelers; and the family members and caregivers who plan trips for them.

The value: we combine the Korea Tourism Organization's official barrier-free facility dataset (over 10,000 verified locations) with first-hand reviews from travelers, and we rank those reviews so that reviews written by people with the SAME accessibility needs appear first. A wheelchair user sees wheelchair-relevant experiences before anything else.

■ 3. SETUP AND ACCESS

No account is required for the core experience. Browsing places, search, place details, accessibility facility information, and reading reviews all work signed out. An account is only needed to write a post or review, create a travel plan, and save/like items.

Demo account credentials are in the Sign-In Information fields of App Review Information. There is only ONE account type — there are no admin, business, or premium tiers.

First launch — three onboarding screens, no sign-up required:
1. Welcome screen ("안녕하세요"). Tap the button to continue.
2. "계획에 앞서, 여행자님을 위해 저희가 배려할 요소가 있다면 알려주세요" — you must select at least one option before the button enables. Select 지체장애 (mobility / wheelchair) to see our main feature, or 해당없음 (Not applicable) to skip. Then tap 완료했어요 (Done).
3. Tap 둘러보기 (Browse) to enter the app without signing in.

To sign in: Home → gear icon (top right) → 로그인 → "이메일로 시작하기" (Continue with email). Sign in with Apple also works. Kakao sign-in requires the KakaoTalk app, so please use email or Apple. If an email-verification screen appears, tap 나중에 하기 (Do it later) — it blocks nothing, and you do not need access to the demo mailbox.

Key screens:
• Account deletion: 설정 → 프로필 편집 → 회원 탈퇴
• Report / Block: open any post or review → ⋮ menu (top right) → 신고 (Report) / 차단 (Block). Your own content shows 삭제 (Delete) instead.
• Blocked users list: 설정 → 차단한 사용자
• Support: 설정 → 고객센터 → help@moduwa.app
• Accessibility profile: 설정 → 내 무장애정보 편집

No sample files are needed. There is no paid content, no subscription, and no in-app purchase anywhere in the app.

■ 4. EXTERNAL SERVICES

Data provider
• Korea Tourism Organization (한국관광공사) TourAPI — public open data. Source of all place listings, barrier-free facility attributes, and place photography (tong.visitkorea.or.kr). Attribution is displayed in the app at the bottom of 설정.

Our own backend
• A REST API we operate ourselves, hosted on Railway (moduwa-backend-production.up.railway.app), with a PostgreSQL database. It stores accounts, reviews, posts, comments, travel plans, and user-uploaded photos. Photos are stored on our own server volume; no third-party storage service is used.

Authentication
• Sign in with Apple
• Google Sign-In (accounts.google.com, oauth2.googleapis.com)
• Kakao Login (kakao-ios-sdk)

Maps
• Kakao Maps SDK — renders the map on place detail and travel plan screens.

Notifications
• Apple Push Notification service (APNs). Permission is requested only when the user turns the notification toggle on at 설정 → 알림 설정; nothing is asked at launch.

Not used: no payment processor (the app is free with no in-app purchases), no AI or machine-learning service, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no user tracking. The app's privacy manifest declares NSPrivacyTracking = false, and so do all three bundled third-party components.

The app requests no location, camera, or photo-library permission. Photos are attached through the SwiftUI PhotosPicker, which requires no permission prompt.

■ 5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES

The app is distributed in South Korea only. All content is in Korean, and the underlying dataset covers South Korean destinations exclusively. There is no region-gated feature, no region-specific pricing (the app is free), and no content that varies by country or region.

■ 6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL

The app does not operate in a regulated industry. It is a travel information and community app. It provides no medical, diagnostic, or treatment information and is not a medical device: accessibility attributes such as "ramp at the entrance" or "accessible restroom" describe a building's facilities, not a person's health or care.

Third-party material: place data and photography come from the Korea Tourism Organization TourAPI, published through Korea's national open data portal (data.go.kr) under terms that permit reuse, including commercial reuse, with attribution.
