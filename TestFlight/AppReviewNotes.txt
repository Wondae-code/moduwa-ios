■ WHAT THIS APP IS

moduwa helps people find places in South Korea they can actually get into — listings say a place exists, but not whether there is a ramp, an elevator, or a step at the entrance. We combine the Korea Tourism Organization's official barrier-free dataset (10,000+ locations) with traveler reviews, ranked so reviews by people with the SAME accessibility needs come first.

■ WHAT CHANGED IN 1.1

Trip planning and sign-in only. No new permissions, services or paid content.

• Trip dates can be edited after a plan exists. Shortening a trip drops days outside the new range, so the app counts what would be lost and asks again first.
• The itinerary editor shows one day at a time, with arrows between days. To move a stop to another day, press and hold it and pick a date.
• Tapping a stop's number in the list moves the map to that place, and the region list grew from 12 to 14.
• Fixed: one email address used with email, Google and Kakao sign-in made separate accounts. Those paths now lead to one account.

■ SIGNING IN

Browsing, search, place details, accessibility info and reviews work without an account. Sign-in is only needed to post or review, create a plan, and save/like items. Demo credentials are in the Sign-In Information fields above. There is only ONE account type — no admin, business or premium tiers, no paid content or in-app purchase, no sample files needed.

Onboarding is 3 screens, no sign-up: welcome; accessibility needs (pick at least one to enable the button — 지체장애 = mobility/wheelchair shows our main feature, 해당없음 = Not applicable skips it — then 완료했어요/Done); then 둘러보기 (Browse) to enter.

To sign in: Home → gear icon → 로그인 → "이메일로 시작하기" (Continue with email). Sign in with Apple also works; Kakao needs the KakaoTalk app, so please use email or Apple. If an email-verification screen appears, tap 나중에 하기 (Do it later) — it blocks nothing. Signing in with Google or Kakao on an address that already has an account opens a link dialog; that is the 1.1 fix above and either choice is safe.

All content is in Korean. The app is new, so user-written reviews are still few — expected at launch, not a loading failure.

■ EXTERNAL SERVICES

• Korea Tourism Organization (한국관광공사) TourAPI — public open data: place listings, barrier-free attributes and photography. Attributed at the bottom of 설정.
• Our own REST API on Railway with PostgreSQL — accounts, reviews, posts, comments, plans and user photos, on our own volume.
• Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, Kakao Login. Kakao Maps SDK. APNs for push.

No payment processor, AI service, analytics, advertising SDK or tracking. Our privacy manifest declares NSPrivacyTracking = false, as do all three bundled components.

■ PERMISSIONS

No location, camera or photo-library permission is requested — photos are attached with the SwiftUI PhotosPicker, which needs no prompt. The only one requested is push notifications, and only when the user turns on the toggle at 설정 → 알림 설정. Nothing is asked at launch.

■ REGIONAL DIFFERENCES

None. South Korea only, identical everywhere available, no region-gated features, and the app is free.

■ REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY

Not a regulated industry — a travel and community app, not a medical device: "ramp at the entrance" describes a building, not a person's health. TourAPI data comes from Korea's open data portal (data.go.kr), which permits reuse with attribution; we show that in-app and can supply documentation. Other content is ours or user-submitted under our Terms.
