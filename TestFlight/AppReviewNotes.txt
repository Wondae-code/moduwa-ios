■ WHAT THIS APP IS

moduwa helps people find travel destinations in South Korea they can actually get into — public listings say a place exists, but not whether there is a ramp, an elevator, or a step at the entrance. We combine the Korea Tourism Organization's official barrier-free dataset (10,000+ verified locations) with traveler reviews, ranked so reviews by people with the SAME accessibility needs appear first.

Audience: travelers with mobility, visual or hearing impairments; families with strollers; older travelers; and those who plan trips for them.

■ WORKS WITHOUT SIGNING IN

Browsing, search, place details, accessibility information and reading reviews all work without an account. Sign-in is only needed to write a post or review, create a plan, and save/like items. Demo credentials are in the Sign-In Information fields above. Only ONE account type — no admin, business or premium tiers. No sample files needed, and no paid content, subscription or in-app purchase.

■ FIRST LAUNCH — 3 onboarding screens, no sign-up

1. Welcome screen — tap the button to continue.
2. Accessibility-needs screen — you must select at least one option before the button enables. Select 지체장애 (mobility / wheelchair) to see our main feature, or 해당없음 (Not applicable) to skip. Then tap 완료했어요 (Done).
3. Tap 둘러보기 (Browse) to enter without signing in.

To sign in: Home → gear icon (top right) → 로그인 → "이메일로 시작하기" (Continue with email). Sign in with Apple also works; Kakao needs the KakaoTalk app, so please use email or Apple. If an email-verification screen appears, tap 나중에 하기 (Do it later) — it blocks nothing, and you do not need the demo mailbox.

■ WHERE TO FIND THINGS

All under the gear icon at the top right of Home, which opens 설정 (Settings).

• Account deletion (5.1.1(v)): 설정 → 프로필 편집 → 회원 탈퇴
• Report / Block: any post or review → ⋮ menu (top right) → 신고 / 차단. Your own content shows 삭제 (Delete).
• Blocked users: 설정 → 차단한 사용자
• Support (1.2): 설정 → 고객센터 → help@moduwa.app · https://moduwa.app/support
• Privacy policy: https://moduwa.app/privacy · Terms: 설정 → 서비스 이용약관
• Main feature: set your needs at 설정 → 내 무장애정보 편집, then set the Home sort to 추천 (Recommended).

All content is in Korean. This is a new app, so the number of user-written reviews is still small — expected at launch, not a loading failure.

■ EXTERNAL SERVICES

• Korea Tourism Organization (한국관광공사) TourAPI — public open data: all place listings, barrier-free attributes and place photography. Attributed at the bottom of 설정.
• Our own REST API on Railway with PostgreSQL — accounts, reviews, posts, comments, plans and user photos. Photos sit on our own server volume; no third-party storage.
• Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, Kakao Login. Kakao Maps SDK for maps. APNs for push.

Not used: no payment processor, AI service, analytics SDK, advertising SDK or tracking. Our privacy manifest declares NSPrivacyTracking = false, as do all three bundled third-party components.

■ PERMISSIONS

No location, camera or photo-library permission is requested — photos are attached through the SwiftUI PhotosPicker, which needs no prompt. The only permission requested is push notifications, and only when the user turns the toggle on at 설정 → 알림 설정. Nothing is asked at launch.

■ REGIONAL DIFFERENCES

None. Distributed in South Korea only; behaviour is identical everywhere available. No region-gated features, no region-specific pricing (the app is free).

■ REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY

Not a regulated industry — a travel information and community app. No medical, diagnostic or treatment information, and not a medical device: attributes like "ramp at the entrance" describe a building, not a person's health. TourAPI data comes from Korea's national open data portal (data.go.kr) under terms permitting reuse with attribution, which we display in-app; documentation on request. All other content is written by us or submitted by our users under our Terms of Service.
